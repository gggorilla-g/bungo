【タイトル】
Flip One Square Over All Four Edges, Again and Again: 78,125 Squares #Shorts #fractal #satisfying

【説明】
One square is flipped over its top, right, bottom and left edges, making four copies. The new cross shape is flipped over its four edges again, and again: 1 → 5 → 25 → … → 78,125 squares. The result is a cross made of smaller crosses (the Vicsek fractal).

- Each round multiplies the count by 5 and the size by 3
- Colours are a rainbow set by position
- Sounds: a stone-like tap when each copy lands, and a soft "shh" while it flips. Canon chord progression
- At the end it rewinds at high speed and loops back to the start

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
