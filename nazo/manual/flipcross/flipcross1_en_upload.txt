【タイトル】
Keep Flipping One Square and It Becomes a Lattice of 820,125 #Shorts #fractal #satisfying

【説明】
One square is flipped into all 8 neighbouring spots (over its 4 edges and 4 corners). Then the new shape is flipped into the 4 spots above, below, left and right. Alternating 8 and 4: 1 → 9 → 45 → 405 → … → 820,125 squares, a lattice made of smaller lattices.

- Each round triples the size; the count grows by 9 and 5 in turn
- Colours are a rainbow set by position
- Sounds: a stone-like tap when each copy lands, and a quiet "shh" while it flips. Canon chord progression
- At the end it rewinds at high speed and loops back to the start

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
