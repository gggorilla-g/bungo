【タイトル】
What If Your Running Speed Doubled Every Second? #shorts

【説明】
What if your running speed doubled every second? A 3D thought experiment.

Numbers:
- Start at 5 m/s (11 mph), doubling every second: 10 m/s at 1 s, 20 m/s at 2 s, 160 m/s at 5 s, 320 m/s at 6 s, 640 m/s at 7 s, 1,280 m/s at 8 s, 10,240 m/s at 11 s, 20,480 m/s at 12 s (calculation).
- The 100 m world record (9.58 s) averages about 10.4 m/s (World Athletics).
- Japan's fastest bullet trains run at 320 km/h (199 mph) in service. Jet airliners cruise at roughly 900 km/h (560 mph).
- Speed of sound at 15 C is about 340 m/s, so 640 m/s is about Mach 1.9 and 1,280 m/s about Mach 3.8. Rifle bullets travel roughly 900 m/s, about 2,000 mph (varies by gun and round).
- Orbital speed near Earth is about 7.9 km/s (4.9 mi/s) and escape speed about 11.2 km/s (7 mi/s) (JAXA). Air and terrain are ignored in this thought experiment.
- Air temperature on impact uses stagnation temperature T = T0 + v^2/(2cp) (T0 = 288 K, cp = 1005 J/kg/K): about 1,100 K, or roughly 830 C / 1,500 F, at 1,280 m/s (estimate).
- When speed doubles every second, acceleration is speed x about 0.69: about 890 m/s^2, or 90 g, at 1,280 m/s (calculation).
- Distances and sizes are not to scale.

Sources

- JAXA (orbital and escape speed) https://www.jaxa.jp/projects/pr/brochure/pdf/08/kids_02.pdf
