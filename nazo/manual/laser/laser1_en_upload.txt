【タイトル】
The Light Splits Every Marble It Crosses. Will It Reach 2048? #Shorts #physics #satisfying

【説明】
The light line hangs from springs on two rails. A motor slowly shakes the top anchors, the springs stretch and bounce, and the line moves up and down.
Every marble the line crosses splits into two. It stops at 2048.

- The carriage mass, spring stiffness and air drag are solved every 1/960 s
- The motor speeds up over time; near the spring's natural frequency the swing grows (resonance)
- Every split plays one note, and the notes rise as the count grows

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
