【タイトル】
Drop the Digits of Pi as Balls. Which Digit Reaches 24 First? #Shorts #pi #math

【説明】
The digits of pi after 3.14159… fall one by one as balls into tubes 0 to 9. The first digit to collect 24 balls wins.

- These are the real digits of pi (up to the 186th decimal place)
- 8 leads for most of the race, but stalls at 23. Meanwhile 2 catches up and reaches 24 first at the 186th digit
- Each sound plays when a ball lands. Pitch follows the digit (0 low, 9 high), over the Canon chord progression

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
