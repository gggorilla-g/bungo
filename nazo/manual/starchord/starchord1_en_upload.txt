【タイトル】
Light balls bouncing inside a circle. Can 10 of them make one chord? #Shorts #math #relaxing

【説明】
Balls of light bounce inside a round frame. Each path traces a star, and every bounce rings a glass note.

- Inside a circle the ball leaves the wall at the same angle it hit it, so every bounce moves it the same angle around the center. If each bounce moves it 360° x q/p, it returns to its start after p bounces and draws a star with p points (5/2 gives a pentagram).
- Balls are added two at a time. The second one is the mirror image of the first (going the other way) and plays one octave higher.
- The five stars are 5/2, 7/3, 8/3, 9/4 and 12/5. The notes are D, A, F#, E and B, which sound clean in any combination.
- Once all 10 are moving, the tempo speeds up at the very end, and all 10 reach the edge at the same moment to ring one chord.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made with Python)
