Same Stars Merge Into a Heavier One. How Many Suns Make a Black Hole? #Shorts #space #blackhole

【説明】
Suns drop one at a time. When two stars of the same mass touch, they merge into one heavier star (1, 2, 4, 8, 16).
The 32nd Sun turns the pile into a black hole. After that it swallows every Sun that falls in, and it keeps getting heavier and bigger.

- Merging by doubling is a rule of this video only
- The masses follow real stars: Sirius is about 2 Suns, Regulus about 4, Bellatrix about 8, Betelgeuse about 10 to 20
- Stars heavier than about 20 Suns are thought to be able to end their lives as black holes (where exactly the line is, is still uncertain)
- A black hole's radius is proportional to its mass (the Schwarzschild radius). At 512 Suns it is 16 times the radius at 32 Suns
- The warped view around the black hole is a drawing of light being bent, not an exact calculation
- Each sound plays at the moment stars touch or get swallowed. Bigger stars sound lower
