【タイトル】
Drops Into Six Liquids of Different Weights. Each Sinks to Its Own Layer and Rings #Shorts #relaxing #liquid

【説明】
Six liquids of different densities are stacked in a glass. Drops fall one by one, pass through the lighter layers, stop at the layer of their own liquid, and ring as they merge.

- Densities (g/cm3, approximate): alcohol 0.79, oil 0.92, water 1.00, dish soap 1.06, maple syrup 1.33, honey 1.42. Lighter liquids float on top (values vary by brand)
- The smaller the density difference across a boundary, the slower and larger it sways (the speed of waves along a boundary is proportional to the square root of the difference divided by the sum)
- Heavier liquids play lower notes. The six layers are six notes of a chord, following the Canon progression
- At the end, one drop of each liquid falls at once. The lightest lands first, so the notes roll from high to low
- Drop motion and wave sizes are adjusted for easy viewing

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
