【タイトル】
Pachelbel's Canon Chords, Played by Falling Balls. All Together Again After 18 s #Shorts #music #physics

【説明】
Ten balls bounce on wooden pads. Each pad plays a note of the current chord of the Canon progression (D A Bm F#m G D G A).
They start together, then drift apart into waves.

How it works:
- In 18 seconds the leftmost ball bounces 12 times, the next 13 times, and the rightmost 21 times
- So they slowly drift apart; at 9 seconds every other ball lines up
- At 18 seconds every ball has bounced a whole number of times, so all of them land together, just as the chords come back to D
- Bounce height is proportional to the square of the bounce period (same gravity). The wooden pillars show that height
- The sound uses three wood-like tones (wood, bamboo, hollow wood), one per pad in turn
