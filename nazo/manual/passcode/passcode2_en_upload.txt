Every Tap Is a Deep Bass Hit. It Gets Faster Until the Drop (Use Headphones) #shorts #bass #music

【説明】
Each key press plays one deep bass hit. The taps speed up: quarter notes, eighth notes, sixteenths, then thirty-seconds. The bass drops out, a short silence, and the four-on-the-floor beat comes back. Headphones or speakers recommended.

How it works:
- Tempo: 126 BPM.
- Each tap is an 808-style bass: the pitch drops a little at the start, so it sounds like "doom".
- Lower notes light the lower keys, higher notes the upper keys. The "0" key flashes on the beat.
- Every bass hit pushes the other sounds down for a moment (sidechain), which makes the mix pump.

- The screen is an original drawing, not a recording of a real phone.
- Sound and visuals: original (made with Python).
