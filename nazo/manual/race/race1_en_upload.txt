【タイトル】
Doubling Survival: 16 Colors, Which One Is Left? | Relaxing Physics Marble Race

【説明】
16 colors of marbles roll through the pegs into the boxes at the bottom.
x2: the marbles in that box double next stage. OUT: they are removed. At the end of each stage, the color with the fewest marbles is out (2 colors while 9 or more are left; on a tie, the color whose last marble landed later is out).
Pick one color before you watch.

Every peg hit plays a note from the Canon chord progression. Left is low, right is high.
The boxes change every stage. The more marbles there are, the more OUT boxes appear, so the count never explodes.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
