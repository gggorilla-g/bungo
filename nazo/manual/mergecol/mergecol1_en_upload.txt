Two Same Planets Merge Into a Bigger One. Keep Dropping Moons. Get a Sun? #Shorts #space #canon

【説明】
Moons drop one by one. When two of the same meet, they merge into the next one.
Moon → Mercury → Mars → Venus → Earth → Neptune → Uranus → Saturn → Jupiter → Sun

How it works:
- The drops speed up every bar: quarter notes, then eighths, then sixteenths...
- Every merge plays one note of Pachelbel's Canon. Bigger planets play lower notes.
- The merge rule is only for this video. The order follows real size (Moon smallest, Sun largest), but two real planets do not make the next one.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python). Music: arrangement of Pachelbel's Canon (public domain)
