【タイトル】
Balls Merge When They Touch. Which Color Is Left at the End? #Shorts #physics #simulation

【説明】
240 balls in a circle merge into one when they touch (their areas add up). The bigger one keeps its color.
The fewer balls are left, the faster they move. Which color will the last ball be?

- Sounds play the moment balls merge and when a big ball hits the wall. Bigger balls sound lower

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
