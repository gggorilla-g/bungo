【タイトル】
Can the Black Hole Eat Them All? Blue Balls Multiply at Every Wall Hit #Shorts #blackhole #simulation

【説明】
Blue balls and a black hole compete inside a circle (rules made for this video).

- Blue balls move at a constant speed. Each wall hit spawns a new ball at that spot (only if there is room)
- The black hole swallows any ball it touches and grows by that amount. The bigger it gets, the slower it moves
- The blue balls peak at 118, but in the end the black hole swallows them all
- Sounds play when a blue ball hits the wall (higher spots sound higher) and when a ball is swallowed. Canon chord progression with a soft heartbeat underneath
- At the end it rewinds at high speed and loops back to the start

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
