【タイトル】
Can the Black Hole Eat Them All Before Blue Reaches 60? #Shorts #blackhole #simulation

【説明】
Blue balls and a black hole compete inside a circle (rules made for this video).

- Blue balls fall under gravity and bounce off the circular wall. Every wall bounce, and every blue-blue collision, adds a new blue ball at the centre (only if the centre is free)
- The black hole moves in straight lines at a constant slow speed and turns only at the wall. It swallows any ball it touches and grows a little each time. Nearby balls are pulled in slightly
- Blue wins at 60 balls, black wins at 0. The black hole pushes them back once, but in the end blue reaches 60
- Sounds play when a blue ball hits the wall (higher spots sound higher) and when a ball is swallowed. Canon chord progression
- At the end it rewinds at high speed and loops back to the start

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
