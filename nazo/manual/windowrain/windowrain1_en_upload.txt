【タイトル】
A Rainy Night Car Window: Every Time Two Drops Join, a Note Rings #Shorts #rain #relaxing

【説明】
A car window on a rainy night. Each time two raindrops touch and join, a note rings.

- Touching drops merge into one. Bigger drops play lower notes (Canon chord progression)
- Drops that grow heavy slide down, leaving tiny droplets behind and collecting others on the way
- Each drop works like a lens, so the city lights behind appear upside down inside it
- At the end the rain gets heavier and the wiper sweeps the glass once
- Soft rain sounds sit underneath. Drop counts and sizes are adjusted for easy viewing

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
