【タイトル】
Two of the Same Merge Into the Next Planet. Which One Reaches Final Form? #shorts #satisfying #space

【説明】
Small asteroids drop in from the top. When two of the same kind touch, they merge into the next one up: asteroid, Moon, Mercury, Mars, Venus, Earth, Neptune, Uranus, Saturn, Jupiter, Sun. How far will it get?

How it works:
- Each merge takes two of the same, so reaching level k takes 2 to the power k asteroids (Earth needs 32, the Sun would need 1,024).
- Balls fall under gravity and bounce off each other and the round wall. Where they land is not planned; it depends on how the pile settles.
- Planet sizes here only grow step by step; they are not to scale.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
