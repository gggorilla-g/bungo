【タイトル】
3 Swings Across, 5 Swings Up. Will the Line Close? #shorts #math

【説明】
One ball swings 3 times side to side and 5 times up and down in 32 seconds. Leave its trail on screen and the line closes exactly at 32 seconds, back where it started.

How it works:
- The two swing counts are a whole-number ratio (3 : 5), so both motions return to the start together after 32 seconds and the curve closes. This shape is called a Lissajous curve.
- If the ratio is not a ratio of whole numbers (for example sqrt(2) : 1), the line never closes and keeps drawing new paths forever.
- The vertical motion starts a quarter cycle ahead of the horizontal one.
- Every time the ball crosses the vertical or horizontal center line, it plays one note.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
