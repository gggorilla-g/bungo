【タイトル】
Now Triple Each Number. What Shape Appears? #shorts #math

【説明】
Put 100 numbered points (0 to 99) on a circle. From each point, send one ball to the point with triple its number (subtract 100 until it fits; for example 41 goes to 123 - 100 = 23). Doubling gave a heart-like curve with one cusp (a cardioid). Tripling gives a curve with two cusps, called a nephroid.

How it works:
- Every line from point n to point 3n is tangent to a nephroid.
- In general, multiplying by m gives a curve with m - 1 cusps (1 for doubling, 2 for tripling, 3 for multiplying by 4).
- The bright curve inside a cup lit by distant light (parallel rays) is part of a nephroid too.
- Point 50 maps to itself (150 - 100 = 50), so it is skipped.
- Every ball plays one note when it lands. Longer lines play lower notes.
