【タイトル】
A Parabola Sends Every Ball to One Point. What About a Round Bowl? #shorts #satisfying #math

【説明】
Drop balls straight down into a parabola and every one passes through a single point, the focus. Now try a round bowl (half a circle).

How it works:
- A circular mirror does not bring the lines to one point. Lines near the middle cross close to halfway down from the center; lines near the rim cross higher up.
- Where many reflected lines pile up you get a bright curve called a caustic. This one is part of a nephroid.
- It is the same bright heart-shaped line you see of light inside a coffee cup or a ring.
- This blur is called spherical aberration, and it is why telescope mirrors and satellite dishes are parabolas, not circles.
- No gravity here (balls move like light rays) and balls pass through each other. More balls every round: 8, 32, 128, 384.
