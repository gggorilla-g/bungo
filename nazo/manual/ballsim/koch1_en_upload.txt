【タイトル】
The Area Barely Grows. Can the Edge Grow Forever? (Wait for the End) #shorts #satisfying #math

【説明】
Add a small triangle to the middle third of every side, and repeat. The shape is the Koch snowflake. A glowing ball traces the edge once per step and plays a note at every corner.

How it works:
- Each step multiplies the edge length by 4/3 (3, 4, 5.33, 7.11, ...). You can repeat forever, so the edge grows without limit.
- The added triangles shrink so fast that the area never passes 1.6 times (8/5) the first triangle.
- So this shape has a finite area and an infinitely long edge.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
