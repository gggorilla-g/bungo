【タイトル】
A Dark, Sad Chord Played by Falling Balls. They All Land Together After 18 Seconds #shorts #satisfying #music

【説明】
Ten balls bounce on note pads. The notes are C, G, C, Eb, G, Bb, D, Eb, G, Bb (a C minor chord with an added ninth: dark and sad). They start together, then drift apart into waves. Will they ever land together again?

Answer: after 18 seconds, every ball lands at the same moment.

How it works:
- In 18 seconds the left ball bounces 12 times, the next one 13, the next 14, and the last one 21. Each differs by just one bounce.
- So the timing slowly shifts and you see waves and alternating patterns. At 9 seconds, every other ball lands together.
- After 18 seconds every ball has finished a whole number of bounces, so they all land at once and the full chord sounds.
- The height of a bounce grows with the square of its period (same gravity for all). The columns behind show those heights.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
