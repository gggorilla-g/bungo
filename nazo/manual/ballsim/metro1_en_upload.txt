【タイトル】
98 Glass Metronomes and 2 Rebels. Will They All Sync? #shorts #satisfying #physics

【説明】
100 glass metronomes stand on a glass platform. 98 of them tick at 120 BPM. Rebel A ticks at 122 and rebel B at 140. They all start at random. Will they ever sync?

Answer: the 98 and A fall into sync. B never does.

How it works:
- The platform sits on two rollers, so it is free to slide sideways, like a base-isolated building.
- When the metronomes swing, the platform moves a little the other way. Through that motion the metronomes pull on each other. The red mark below shows where the platform is.
- In this video each one is pulled slightly toward the average swing of the group (the Kuramoto model). The more of them are in sync, the stronger the pull.
- If the speed difference is small, the pull wins. A is only 2 BPM off, so it gets pulled in.
- If the difference is large, the pull cannot keep up. B is 20 BPM faster, so it stays off the beat forever.
- The color shows how far each one is from the group. As the gap shrinks, the colors drift toward the same light blue.
- The sound is a glass note every time a needle passes the center. Each metronome has its own pitch, so in sync they make a chord.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
