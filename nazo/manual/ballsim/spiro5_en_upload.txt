【タイトル】
Same Ring, Smaller Gear. What Happens to the Middle? (Wait for the End) #shorts #satisfying #math

【説明】
A small 32-tooth gear rolls inside a 105-tooth ring. A pen in one of the gear's holes draws a flower. Every petal tip plays a stone chime.

How it works:
- A small gear keeps the pen close to the ring, so it never reaches the center. That leaves a round hole in the middle (about 40% of the ring's radius).
- 105 and 32 share no common factor bigger than 1, so the flower gets 105 petals and the line only closes after 32 laps.
- The curve is a hypotrochoid.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
