【タイトル】
Keep Connecting Earth and Venus. What Appears? #shorts #space #math

【説明】
While Earth goes around the Sun 8 times, Venus goes around almost exactly 13 times. Keep drawing a line between the two planets and a five-petal flower appears.

Numbers:
- Orbital periods: Earth 365.256 days, Venus 224.701 days (NASA). 8 x 365.256 = 2922.05 days and 13 x 224.701 = 2921.11 days, about one day apart (calculation).
- Orbit size (semi-major axis): Venus 108.21 million km, Earth 149.60 million km (NASA), so Venus's orbit is about 0.72 times the size of Earth's.
- Both orbits are drawn as circles here and the ratio is set to exactly 13:8 (the real orbits are slightly elliptical and the ratio is not exact).
- Earth and Venus line up on the same side of the Sun 13 - 8 = 5 times in 8 years, which is why the pattern repeats 5 times.
- 610 lines are drawn at equal time steps. Every third line plays one note. Longer lines play lower notes.

Source
- NASA Venus Fact Sheet https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/venusfact.html

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
