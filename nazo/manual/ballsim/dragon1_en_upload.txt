【タイトル】
Fold a Paper Strip in Half 12 Times, Then Open Every Crease to 90° #shorts

【説明】
Fold a long strip of paper in half 12 times, then open every crease to a right angle, and you get this shape: the dragon curve.

How it works:
- Unfolding is the same as repeatedly attaching a copy of the current line, rotated 90 degrees around its end point.
- Each step doubles the segments: 2^12 = 4,096 after 12 folds.
- Segments can touch at corners, but never cross.
- Each time a rotation finishes, the pivot flashes and a note plays.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
