【タイトル】
Multiply Each Number by 4. How Many Cusps Appear? #shorts #math

【説明】
Put 100 numbered points (0 to 99) on a circle. From each point, send one ball to the point with 4 times its number (subtract 100 until it fits). The lines pile up into a flower-like curve with three cusps.

How it works:
- Every line from point n to point 4n is tangent to the same curve (an epicycloid). Draw enough lines and the curve appears.
- Multiplying by m gives m - 1 cusps (1 for a cardioid at m = 2, 2 for a nephroid at m = 3, 3 at m = 4).
- Points that map to themselves are skipped.
- Every ball plays one note when it lands. Longer lines play lower notes.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
