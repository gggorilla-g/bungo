【タイトル】
Bounce inside a circle, moving 160° each time. Does it come back? #Shorts #math

【説明】
A ball bounces perfectly inside a circle. Each bounce moves it 160° around the rim (4/9 of a turn).

How it works:
- 9 steps of 4/9 make exactly 4 turns, so after 9 bounces the ball is back where it started and the star closes.
- A perfect bounce off a circle leaves at the same angle it came in, so every step covers the same angle.
- A new ball joins after each lap, starting a little further around.
- Each bounce plays one note; the pitch depends on where it hits.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made with Python)
