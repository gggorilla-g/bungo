【タイトル】
Only Swap Neighbors. Can 32 Bars Sort Themselves? (Wait for the Final Sweep) #shorts #satisfying #coding

【説明】
32 shuffled bars. One rule: if the bar on your left is taller, swap. Every swap plays the note of the bar that moved (a C major scale), and a final sweep runs up the scale once everything is in order.

How it works:
- Take the bars one at a time from the left and slide each one left until nothing taller is beside it (insertion sort).
- The left part is always in order; each new bar walks to its place.
- The starting order is random. Nothing is staged: it just follows the rule.

Animation and sound: original (Python).
