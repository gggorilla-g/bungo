【タイトル】
Double Each Number. What Shape Appears? #shorts #math #satisfying

【説明】
Put 100 numbered points (0 to 99) on a circle. From each point, send one ball to the point with double its number (subtract 100 if it goes past 99; for example 63 goes to 126 - 100 = 26). The lines left behind pile up into a heart-like curve called a cardioid.

How it works:
- Every line from point n to point 2n is tangent to a cardioid. Draw enough of them and the curve shows up as their envelope.
- These lines are also the paths of light reflected once inside a circle from a source at point 0 (light going from 0 to n bounces toward 2n). The bright curve made inside a round cup by a light at its rim is the same cardioid.
- Every ball plays one note when it lands. Longer lines play lower notes.
