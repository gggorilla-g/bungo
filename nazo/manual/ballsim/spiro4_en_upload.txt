【タイトル】
Roll a Gear Almost Half the Ring. It's Not a Flower Anymore (Wait for the End) #shorts #satisfying #math

【説明】
A 49-tooth gear rolls inside a 100-tooth ring. A pen in one of the gear's holes draws the line. Every time the line reaches the ring, a stone chime plays.

How it works:
- If the gear were exactly half the ring (50 teeth), the pen would just trace the same thin ellipse over and over.
- Take away one tooth (49) and that thin stroke turns a little on every pass.
- 100 and 49 share no common factor bigger than 1, so there are 100 tips and the line only closes after 49 laps.
- The curve is a hypotrochoid.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
