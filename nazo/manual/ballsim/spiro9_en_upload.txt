【タイトル】
131 and 49 Teeth. What Appears Inside the Flower? (Wait for the End) #shorts #satisfying #math

【説明】
A 49-tooth gear rolls inside a 131-tooth ring. A pen in one of the gear's holes draws a flower. Every petal tip plays a stone chime. The color changes in the order the line is drawn.

How it works:
- 49/131 is very close to 3/8. So after 8 petals the pen is almost back where it started (about 2.7 degrees short).
- The petals gather in 8 directions and drift a little each time. Where they pile up, an 8-pointed star appears.
- 131 and 49 share no common factor bigger than 1, so there are 131 petals and the line only closes after 49 laps.
- The curve is a hypotrochoid.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
