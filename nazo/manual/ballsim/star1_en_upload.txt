【タイトル】
5 Balls Move in Perfect Symmetry Inside a Star, Until One Turns by 0.1° #shorts

【説明】
5 balls leave the center of a star-shaped room at the same speed, spaced exactly 72° apart, so every path is a rotated copy of the others.

How it works:
- Because the room and the start are both 5-fold symmetric, the 5 paths stay perfect copies of each other.
- At 16 seconds, one ball is turned by about 0.1°.
- The star's inward corners split nearby paths apart, so that tiny turn grows on every bounce until the symmetry is gone.
- Every wall hit lights up and plays a note.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
