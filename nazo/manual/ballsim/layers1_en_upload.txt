【タイトル】
1 Ball vs 512 Layers. It Splits Every 4 Bounces #shorts #satisfying #physics

【説明】
One ball falls onto 512 thin layers. Every bounce breaks one layer. After a ball has bounced 4 times, it splits in two. Every break plays the next note of Pachelbel's Canon.

How it works:
- Bounces lose no speed (gravity, no air resistance). As the floor drops, the balls fall deeper each time.
- A new ball leaves at the same speed but at a slightly tilted angle, so its bounce timing drifts away from the others and the notes start to overlap.
- The count doubles again and again, up to 128 balls, so the layers break faster and faster.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python). Melody: Pachelbel's Canon (public domain).
