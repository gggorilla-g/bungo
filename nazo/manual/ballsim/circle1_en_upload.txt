【タイトル】
Fire Balls From the Center. Do They ALL Come Back? #shorts

【説明】
Balls are fired in every direction from the center of a circle at the same speed. After bouncing, every ball returns to the center at the same moment. Each time they come back, the ball count grows 1.5x (4 to 72).

How it works:
- Every point on the wall is one radius from the center, so center to wall to center is always two radii.
- Each ball hits the wall head-on and comes straight back.
- Every wall hit plays one note.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
