【タイトル】
Roll a Gear Inside a Ring. What Flower Does It Draw? (Wait for the End) #shorts #satisfying #math

【説明】
A 41-tooth gear rolls inside a 96-tooth ring. A pen in one of the gear's holes draws a flower. Every petal tip plays a stone-chime note.

How it works:
- 96 and 41 share no common factor bigger than 1, so the flower gets 96 petals and the line only closes after 41 laps.
- Change the tooth ratio and both the petal count and the number of laps change. The curve is a hypotrochoid.
- The hole you put the pen in (its distance from the gear's center) sets how fat the petals are.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
