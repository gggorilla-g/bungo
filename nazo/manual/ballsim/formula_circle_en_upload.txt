Why Is a Circle's Area πr²? Unroll It Into a Triangle #shorts

【説明】
A circle sliced into 30 thin rings. Each ring is cut and straightened, and the stack turns into a triangle.

How it works:
- Each ring, straightened, is as long as its own circumference (2π times its radius).
- From the outermost ring to the innermost, the lengths shrink evenly, so the stack makes a right triangle.
- Its long side is the full circumference 2πr; its short side is the radius r.
- Triangle area = 2πr × r ÷ 2 = πr². Thinner rings make the triangle exact.
