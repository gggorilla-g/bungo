【タイトル】
30 Balls in a Mushroom-Shaped Room. Some Can Never Leave the Cap #shorts

【説明】
30 balls start from one point inside the cap of a mushroom-shaped room and bounce forever.

How it works:
- In a round cap, a ball keeps the same closest distance to the cap's center on every bounce, even when it hits the flat floor of the cap.
- If that distance is larger than half the stem's width, the ball can never line up with the stem opening. It stays in the cap forever.
- Balls aimed closer to the center can drop into the stem, and from then on their paths turn chaotic.
- This is a known shape in math called the "mushroom billiard": order and chaos in one room.
- Every wall hit lights up and plays a note.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
