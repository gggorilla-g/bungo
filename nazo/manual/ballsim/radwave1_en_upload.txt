【タイトル】
Fire 20 balls every way from the center. When do they all meet again? (watch to the end) #Shorts #physics #math

【説明】
Twenty balls leave the center of a circle at the same moment, in every direction. Only their speeds differ. They form a spiral, scatter, and 26 seconds later they all meet at the center again. Each ball plays its own note when it hits the wall.

How it works:
- A path through the center of a circle bounces straight back through the center.
- In 26 seconds the slowest ball makes 14 round trips, the next 15, and so on up to 33. So after 26 seconds every ball is back at the center.
- Along the way they briefly line up in 2 or 3 groups.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made with Python).
