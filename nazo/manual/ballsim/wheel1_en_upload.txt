Can a Triangle Become a Wheel? Every Full Turn Adds One Side #shorts #satisfying #math

【説明】
A triangle rolls along the ground. Every full turn, it gains one side (3, 4, 5 ... 24 sides). Can a triangle become a wheel?

How it works:
- A polygon rolls by tipping over each corner. Every time a side lands, you hear a note.
- It turns at a steady rate. So in one full turn a triangle lands 3 times, a square 4 times, a pentagon 5 times. The beat gets finer and finer.
- The center lifts every time it goes over a corner. The size of that bump is radius x (1 - cos(180 degrees / number of sides)). That is 50% of the radius for a triangle, 29% for a square, 7.6% for 8 sides and 0.9% for 24 sides.
- It never reaches exactly 0%, but it gets as close as you like. The shape it is heading for is a circle: a wheel.
- The thin lines behind are the paths of the corners.
