【タイトル】
Fire Balls Every Way From One Point. Do They All Go Straight Up? #shorts

【説明】
Inside a parabola-shaped wall, balls leave the glowing point (the focus) in every direction. After one bounce, every ball travels straight up, perfectly parallel, and they all reach the top line at the same moment. Then they come back the same way and meet at the focus again.

How it works:
- A parabola sends any line from its focus out parallel to its axis. This is how car headlights and flashlight reflectors throw a straight beam (a satellite dish does the reverse).
- Every point on a parabola is the same distance from the focus as from a line below it (the directrix), so every path focus -> wall -> top line has the same length. Same speed means same arrival time.
- No gravity here: balls move like light. They pass through each other, and the top line is a mirror.
- More balls each round trip. Every wall hit plays one note.
