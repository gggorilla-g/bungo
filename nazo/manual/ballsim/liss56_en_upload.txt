【タイトル】
5 Swings Across, 6 Swings Up. Will the Line Close? #shorts #math

【説明】
One ball swings 5 times side to side and 6 times up and down in 24 seconds. Leave its trail on screen and the line closes exactly at 24 seconds, back where it started.

How it works:
- The two swing counts are a whole-number ratio (5 : 6), so both motions return to the start together after 24 seconds and the curve closes. This shape is called a Lissajous curve.
- If the ratio is not a ratio of whole numbers, the line never closes.
- The vertical motion starts a tenth of a cycle ahead of the horizontal one.
- The ball plays one note every time it crosses a center line and every time it turns around at an edge.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
