Spiral the Numbers and Light Up Only the Primes #shorts #math #primes

【説明】
Put 1 in the middle and write 2, 3, 4 ... in a spiral. Light up only the prime numbers (numbers divisible only by 1 and themselves) and they seem to line up along diagonals.

Numbers:
- 29 x 29 = 841 cells. There are 146 primes up to 841 (calculated).
- The mathematician Stanislaw Ulam noticed this pattern in 1963. It is called the Ulam spiral.
- Numbers along a diagonal follow a quadratic formula in n. Some quadratics are known to produce many primes, but why they do is still a conjecture. This video shows the result up to 841 and does not prove anything beyond that.
- It starts slowly and speeds up. Prime cells play a higher note; the other cells play a low, quiet one.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
