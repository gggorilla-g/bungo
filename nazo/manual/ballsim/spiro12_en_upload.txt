【タイトル】
Roll Four Gears at Once. Each Draws 12 Petals. What Do They Make Together? #shorts #satisfying #math

【説明】
Inside a ring with 96 teeth, gears with 8, 40, 56 and 88 teeth roll at the same time. There are 10 pens in all. What pattern do they make together?

Answer: a border frill, a 12-pointed star, a 12-petal flower and a small ring in the middle, nested inside each other.

How it works:
- The number of petals is the ring's teeth divided by the greatest common divisor. 96 shares 8 with each of 8, 40, 56 and 88, so every gear draws 12 petals.
- Because the petal counts match, the shapes never clash. They form one pattern with 12-fold symmetry.
- A small gear draws near the ring and a big gear draws near the middle. So the pattern splits into bands from outside to inside.
- Each gear uses 2 or 3 pen holes, stacking the same shape at slightly different sizes.
- Each gear is shown as a thin colored circle. It rolls without slipping, and the gears pass through each other.
- The point where a gear touches the ring glows, and a freshly drawn line glows white before it settles into its color.
- When the drawing is done, a wave of light runs from the middle outward and the whole pattern lights up.
