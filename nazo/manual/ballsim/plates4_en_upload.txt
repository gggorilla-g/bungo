【タイトル】
Which One Breaks 10 Plates First? 1000 vs 2ⁿ vs n⁴ vs Fibonacci #shorts #math

【説明】
Four rules drop balls onto 10 plates. The first lane to break all 10 wins. Every round n, the number of balls and the power of each ball change.

Rules (balls dropped in round n):
- 1000: 1,000 balls every round, power 1 each
- 2ⁿ: 2ⁿ balls, power 1 each (2, 4, 8, 16 ...)
- n⁴: n² balls, power n² each (n⁴ in total per round)
- fib²: F(n) balls, power F(n) each, where F is the Fibonacci sequence (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 ...)

Plate strength (top to bottom): 300, 600, 1000, 1500, 2200, 3000, 4000, 5000, 5900, 6500 (30,000 in total)

Result (calculated):
- "1000 every round" leads through round 7 (7,000 total). n⁴ passes it in round 8 (8,772 total).
- n⁴ has 25,333 after round 10 and reaches 30,000 during round 11.
- Doubling (2ⁿ) is last at round 11. With thicker plates it would come back: its running total passes n⁴ in round 18.

Sound: every ball plays one note when it hits a plate. Deeper plates play higher notes.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
