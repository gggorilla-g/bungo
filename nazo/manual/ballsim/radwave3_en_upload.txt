【タイトル】
24 balls fired every way from the center, each linked to its 7th neighbor. What shape is left? #Shorts #math #physics

【説明】
Twenty-four balls leave the center of a circle at once, in every direction. Only their speeds differ. Each ball is linked to the ball seven places away. Whenever they line up in 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 groups, that shape is kept. After 26 seconds every ball is back at the center.

How it works:
- A path through the center of a circle bounces straight back through the center.
- In 26 seconds the slowest ball makes 16 round trips, the next 17, and so on up to 39.
- Each ball plays its own note when it hits the wall.
- Time slows down around the moments when the balls line up in 2, 3 or 4 groups, and speeds up in between. Only the playback speed changes, not the motion.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made with Python).
