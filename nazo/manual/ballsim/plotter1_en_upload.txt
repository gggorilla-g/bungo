【タイトル】
A Printer Prints It Line by Line. What Picture Appears? #shorts

【説明】
A print head sweeps back and forth, printing one line at a time. The picture is the Mandelbrot set.

How it works:
- For each point c on the plane, z is replaced by z*z + c over and over. Points that never escape are dark; points that escape are colored by how many steps it took.
- Points near the edge take longer to escape, so they glow brightest.
- Each time the head turns around, one note plays; crossing the edge of the set adds a softer note.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
