【タイトル】
Every Gold Hit = 1 New Ball. How Long Until It Bursts? #shorts

【説明】
Every time a ball passes through a gold point, one new ball is added. More balls means more gold hits, so the count keeps growing faster and faster. At 500 balls, the ring bursts.

How it works:
- 2D physics simulation: gravity, perfectly elastic ring wall, ball-to-ball collisions.
- Each ball that passes a gold point creates one new ball (one per pass).
- Every wall hit plays one note of the current chord, so the sound gets denser as the balls multiply.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
