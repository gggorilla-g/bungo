【タイトル】
Each Ball Is One Note. Name This Christmas Song (Listen to the End) #shorts #satisfying #christmas

【説明】
Balls travel around five rings at the same speed. Every time a ball crosses the line on the right, its ring plays a note. Outer rings are higher. Can you name the Christmas song?

Answer: Jingle Bells (written in 1857, public domain).

How it works:
- The rings from inside to outside are C, D, E, F, G. One ball is one note.
- All balls move at the same speed, so their positions on the rings are the sheet music. It works like a disc music box.
- A ball a quarter turn away from the line plays a quarter turn later.
- The first lap is slow and the second lap is fast. The second lap ends a little differently.
- At the end, all five rings light up together.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
