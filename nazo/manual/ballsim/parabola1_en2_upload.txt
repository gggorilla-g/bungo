【タイトル】
Drop Balls Straight Down Into a Bowl. Do They All Cross One Point? #shorts

【説明】
Balls fall straight down into a parabola-shaped wall. After one bounce, every ball passes through the same point (the focus), then bounces again and heads straight back up.

How it works:
- A parabola reflects any line parallel to its axis through its focus. Satellite dishes and reflecting telescopes use the same idea.
- Here each ball has a slightly different speed, so they reach the focus one after another instead of all at once (at equal speeds they would arrive together).
- No gravity: the balls move like light and pass through each other. The top line is a mirror.
- More balls every round trip. Each wall hit plays a note on a C major scale over a gentle chord progression and beat.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
