【タイトル】
Fire Balls From One Point. Do They ALL Meet at the Other? #shorts

【説明】
Inside an ellipse, balls are fired in every direction from one focus. After bouncing off the wall, every ball passes through the other focus at the same moment. Each time they come back, the ball count grows 1.5x (4 to 72).

How it works:
- An ellipse reflects any line from one focus through the other focus.
- Focus to wall to focus is the same length in every direction (the long diameter), so at the same speed they all arrive together.
- Every wall hit plays one note.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
