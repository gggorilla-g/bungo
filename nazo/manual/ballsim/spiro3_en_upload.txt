【タイトル】
Roll a 45-Tooth Gear Inside a 99-Tooth Ring. How Many Petals? #shorts #satisfying #math

【説明】
A 45-tooth gear rolls inside a 99-tooth ring. A pen in one of the gear's holes draws a flower. Every petal tip plays a stone-chime note.

How it works:
- The greatest common factor of 99 and 45 is 9, so the flower gets 99/9 = 11 petals and the line closes after 45/9 = 5 laps.
- The curve is a hypotrochoid. The hole you put the pen in sets how fat the petals are.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
