【タイトル】
Can You Name This Song? Every Bounce Plays One Note (Before 64 Balls) #shorts #satisfying #physics

【説明】
Balls fall straight down into a parabola-shaped wall. Every wall hit plays the next note of a famous tune. The ball count doubles every round (4, 8, 16, 32, 64), so it keeps getting faster. Can you name it?

How it works:
- A parabola reflects every line parallel to its axis through its focus, so every ball crosses the same point.
- The wall is higher toward the sides, so the hits are naturally spread out in time. That spread is the rhythm you hear.
- No gravity here (it moves like light).

Answer: Beethoven, Symphony No. 9, "Ode to Joy" (public domain).

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
