【タイトル】
64 Balls Start Together in a D-Shaped Room #shorts

【説明】
64 balls leave the middle of the flat wall together, fanned out like a rainbow.

How it works:
- A circle is a calm room: paths repeat in neat patterns.
- Cut the circle with one straight wall and it becomes a D shape. Now neighboring paths drift apart after each bounce off the flat wall.
- The tidy fan spreads out until the balls fill the whole room.
- Every wall hit lights up and plays a note.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
