【タイトル】
15 Pendulums, Only the Length Changes. Watch Them Line Up Again #shorts

【説明】
15 pendulums with slightly different lengths, released together from the same angle. They drift into waves, look random for a while, then all line up again.

How it works:
- For small swings, a pendulum's period is about T = 2π√(L/g). Mass is not in the formula.
- 4x the length means 2x the period.
- Here the lengths are chosen so that in one cycle the first pendulum swings 26 times, the next 27, and so on. After that cycle, they all meet again.
- Galileo noticed the swing time barely depends on the swing size; Huygens built the first pendulum clock in 1656.
- Every time a pendulum reaches the right end, it plays a soft note.
