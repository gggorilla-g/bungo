【タイトル】
Every Bounce Adds a Brick. Name the Song Before It's Full #shorts #satisfying #physics

【説明】
A ball flies inside a circle. Every time it hits the wall, a brick sticks where it hit. The bricks pile up inward, the space shrinks, the bounces come faster, and so does the tune. Every bounce plays the next note of a well-known melody. Can you name it?

Answer: "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star" (traditional French melody, public domain).

How it works:
- The ball moves in straight lines and bounces off the wall at the same angle it came in.
- Each brick is laid exactly where the ball hit, so the shape of the wall is drawn by the ball itself.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
