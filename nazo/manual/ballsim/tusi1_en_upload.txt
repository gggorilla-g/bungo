【タイトル】
Roll a half-size circle inside a circle. What path do the dots draw? #Shorts #math

【説明】
A circle with exactly half the radius rolls without slipping inside a bigger circle. Every dot on its rim looks like it is circling, but each one only moves back and forth along a straight diameter of the big circle (the Tusi couple).

How it works:
- When the small circle's center goes around by angle θ, the small circle itself turns back by θ. Together, a rim dot stays on one straight line.
- Any rim dot gives a straight line; a different dot gives a different direction.
- With many dots moving at once, the group looks like a rotating circle.
- Each dot plays one note when it reaches the big circle.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made with Python)
