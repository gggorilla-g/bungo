【タイトル】
One Ball Flows In and Multiplies. How Many Balls to Break All 255 Glass? #shorts #satisfying #math

【説明】
255 pieces of glass sit along a spiral track. One ball at a time flows in from outside. When it passes the point, it multiplies: x1, x2, x4, x8... (the multiplier doubles every time). Each ball breaks one piece. How many balls have to flow in to break it all?

How it works:
- Eight balls flow in. The multipliers are x1, x2, x4, x8, x16, x32, x64, x128.
- 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + 16 + 32 + 64 + 128 = 255, so the very last ball from the eighth pass breaks the very last piece.
- Add up eight doublings starting from 1 and you always get one less than the next number (256 - 1 = 255).
- The glass breaks at a steady pace, so you hear rolls of 1, 2, 4, 8... hits. Each hit plays a note from the current chord, going up and down.
- Balls pass through each other.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
