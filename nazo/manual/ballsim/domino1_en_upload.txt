【タイトル】
Each Domino Is 1.5x Bigger. Can the Tiny One Topple the Giant? #shorts #satisfying #physics

【説明】
14 dominoes, each one 1.5 times taller than the one before. The first is tiny; the last is about 195 times taller. Can the chain make it all the way?

How it works:
- A falling domino can knock over a domino up to about 1.5 times its own size. This was worked out by physicist Lorne Whitehead in 1983, and it has been shown with real dominoes many times.
- Each step multiplies the size, so 13 steps of 1.5x make the last one 1.5^13, about 195 times taller than the first.
- Bigger dominoes fall more slowly (fall time grows with the square root of height), so the beat stretches out and the notes get lower as the giants fall.

Note: this is a simple animation of the idea, not a full physics simulation.
