【タイトル】
Three Stars Released From Rest. What Happens? #shorts

【説明】
Three stars with masses 3, 4 and 5 start at rest on the corners of a 3-4-5 right triangle. Only gravity acts. They swing past each other again and again, until one gets thrown out.

How it works:
- This setup is the classic "Pythagorean three-body problem". The start is fixed, yet the motion can't be predicted far ahead (chaos).
- The stars only pull on each other: no air, no collisions.
- Each time two stars reach their closest point, one glass-like note plays.
- Bright lines are the recent paths, faint lines are older ones.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python).
