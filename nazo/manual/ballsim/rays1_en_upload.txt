【タイトル】
Send 1000 Rays Into a Circle. Do Any Hit You in the Middle? #shorts #satisfying #physics

【説明】
Parallel rays of light enter a circle and bounce off the wall. 10 rays, then 100, then 1000. You are standing in the middle. Does any ray hit you?

Answer: not one. The rays keep running around near the wall and a dark circle stays in the middle.

How it works:
- Inside a circle, a ray travels the same distance between bounces and reflects at the same angle every time.
- So a ray that first passes at distance x from the center stays touching a circle of radius x forever. It can never go inside it.
- In this video the innermost ray passes at half the radius. So no ray ever enters the inner half.
- This is the same reason a whisper near the wall of a big dome can be heard far along the wall: a whispering gallery.
- With more rays the lines overlap and start to look like silk.
