【タイトル】
How Many Folds to Reach the Moon? #shorts

【説明】
Paper is 0.1 mm (0.004 in) thick. Each fold doubles it. How many folds until the stack reaches the Moon?

Answer: 42 folds.

*A thought experiment. In real life, the record is 12 folds (Britney Gallivan, 2002, with a 4,000 ft strip of thin paper).
*Earth and Moon are enlarged so you can see them.

Sources: NASA Moon Facts (average distance 238,855 miles), FAI (Karman line, 100 km), ESA (geostationary orbit, 35,786 km), Guinness World Records.
Simulation and animation: original.
