【タイトル】
What if every jump went 10x higher? #shorts

【説明】
Jump 1 is 50 cm (1.6 ft). Every jump goes 10 times higher. Which jump clears Tokyo Tower, Mt. Fuji, and reaches space?
*A thought experiment. Earth gravity only, no air resistance. Takeoff G assumes a 50 cm (20 in) push-off. The figure is enlarged for visibility.
