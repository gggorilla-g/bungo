【タイトル】
Everest vs Olympus Mons (Mars) Size Comparison #shorts

【説明】
From a person to Tokyo Tower, Tokyo Skytree, Burj Khalifa, Mt. Fuji, Mt. Everest and Olympus Mons on Mars, lined up by height.
*Olympus Mons is often given as about 22 to 27 km depending on how it is measured; this uses NASA JPL's figure of about 27 km. Mountain widths are compressed to fit the screen; shapes and colors are approximate. The person is 1.7 m (5 ft 7 in).

- Tokyo Skytree 634 m: GO TOKYO https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/6/
- Burj Khalifa (Dubai) 828 m: CTBUH https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/burj-khalifa/3
- Mt. Fuji (Japan) 3,776 m: Japan Meteorological Agency https://www.data.jma.go.jp/svd/vois/data/tokyo/STOCK/souran/main/55_Fujisan.pdf
- Mt. Everest 8,848.86 m: Al Jazeera (Nepal/China 2020 announcement) https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2020/12/8/mt-everest-nepal-china-announce-revised-height-at-8848-metres
- Olympus Mons (Mars) about 27,000 m: NASA JPL https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/images/pia00300-olympus-mons/
