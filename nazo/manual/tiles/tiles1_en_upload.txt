【タイトル】
Every Bounce Thins the Tile. Which Color Is Still Bouncing at the End? #Shorts #physics

【説明】
Eight colored balls bounce in turn, all to the same height. The 14 tiles all have different thicknesses (1 to 14).
Every bounce makes the tile one layer thinner. A ball that comes down where no tile is left falls out. Each fall lowers the bounce, so the rest speed up.
Which color is still bouncing at the end? Pick one before you watch.

- Each note plays at the exact moment a ball lands. Tiles on the left are lower, tiles on the right are higher
- Every lap moves one chord forward in the Canon progression. When a ball falls, its note drops out
- The sideways speeds and the tile order are random

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
