What If a Formula's Graph Were the Sound? From #1 to #∞ It Becomes EDM #Shorts #math #EDM

【説明】
The formula on screen is exactly how each sound is made (x is the beat, 128 BPM).

#1 Kick: y = 2e^(−7·mod(x,1)) − 1 sets the loudness of a falling low tone
#2 Bass: y = sgn(sin 2πx) + 0.8·sgn(sin 3πx); its size is the volume and its sign picks the octave (roots of the Canon progression)
#3 Hi-hat: y = e^(−30·mod(x+0.5,1)) sets the loudness of an offbeat noise
#4 Chords: y = sin(2πfx + 2.5 sin 6πfx)·e^(−3·mod(x,2)), where f is a chord note
#∞ All of them: the four play together, ducking under each kick, with an added sub bass. The last bar drops to half tempo
