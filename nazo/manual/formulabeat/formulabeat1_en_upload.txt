【タイトル】
What if a formula's graph were the sound? 7 formulas become an EDM track #Shorts #math #EDM

【説明】
Each formula on screen is exactly how its sound is made (x is the beat, 128 BPM).

#1 Kick: y = 2e^(−7·mod(x,1)) − 1 sets the loudness of a low thump
#2 Bass: y = sgn(sin 2πx) + 0.8·sgn(sin 3πx) sets the volume, and its sign picks the octave (root notes of the Canon progression)
#3 Hi-hat: y = e^(−30·mod(x+0.5,1)) sets the off-beat hiss
#4 Clap: y = e^(−14·mod(x+1,2)) sets the clap on beats 2 and 4
#5 Wobble bass: y = (1 + cos 2πx)/2 sets how muffled the low saw sounds
#6 Arpeggio: y = ⌊4·mod(x,1)⌋/3 picks which of the four chord notes plays
#7 Chords: y = e^(−4·mod(x,4)) is how far the pitch slides down into each chord
#8 Build-up: a clap at x_k = 8 − 16/(k+2). The gaps shrink as it closes in on 8
#∞ Everything: all 7 layered. It builds up once more, drops with everything together, and lands on D to finish

Graphs, sound and animation: original (made with Python)
