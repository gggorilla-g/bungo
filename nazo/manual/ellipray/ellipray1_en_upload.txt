【タイトル】
Can Light Fill an Elliptical Mirror? Every Ray Splits in Two at a Focus #Shorts #ellipse #math

【説明】
One ray of light leaves a focus inside an elliptical mirror. Each time it reaches a focus it splits into two rays at right angles (a rule made for this video). 1 → 2 → 4 → … → 262,144 rays. Every place a ray has passed glows in a rainbow colour set by its position, brighter each time a ray passes again.

- In an ellipse, a ray from one focus always passes through the other focus, wherever it bounces
- The path length is the same in every direction (equal to the long diameter), so all rays reach the next focus at the same moment
- The number of rays doubles every 2.75 seconds, filling the ellipse with rainbow light over 19 stages
- Sounds play when rays hit the mirror and when they meet at a focus (a tone like tapping a slightly hollow stone; higher hits sound higher; when there are too many, only some of them sound). Canon chord progression

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
