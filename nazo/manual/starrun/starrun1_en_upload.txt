【タイトル】
64 Stars Roll Down the Zigzag Slopes. Will They All Land in the Bowl? #Shorts #stars #relaxing

【説明】
64 stars drop one by one from the tube, roll down the zigzag slopes and fall into the half-round bowl below.

- The stars roll under gravity and collide with each other, the slopes and the walls
- The bowl fills up and the last star gets pushed out: 63 of 64 stay in
- A sound plays when a star hits hard (higher spots sound higher). Glass tone with echo, Canon chord progression, a soft heartbeat underneath

Simulation, sound and animation: original (made in Python)
