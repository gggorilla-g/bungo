【タイトル】
6 Rooms, 6 Ways to Bounce: Light Trails in Circle, Ellipse, Square, Triangle, Hexagon and Stadium

【説明】
Small balls of light bounce inside six differently shaped rooms. Every wall hit plays one note and flashes where it lands, and each path leaves a slowly fading trail.

Chapters
0:00 Circle: seven paths draw star shapes, then a tiny nudge makes them drift into rosettes
1:37 Ellipse: balls fired from one focus all pass through the other focus, and split every time they do
3:15 Square: two paths close into loops, one never repeats and slowly fills the room
4:52 Triangle: six balls from the center fold into a kaleidoscope
6:30 Hexagon: a fan of 48 balls from one corner spreads like a wave
8:07 Stadium: two balls that start almost exactly the same drift apart into completely different paths

How it works
- Every reflection follows the mirror rule: the angle in equals the angle out.
- An ellipse sends any line from one focus through the other focus.
- In a square, a path closes only when its slope is a fraction; otherwise it never repeats.
- The stadium shape is chaotic: a difference of one millionth of a radian grows until the two paths share nothing.

Simulation, sound and animation: original (Python). No AI-generated footage.
