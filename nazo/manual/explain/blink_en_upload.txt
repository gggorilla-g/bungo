What If You Never Blinked? #shorts

【説明】
What would happen to your eyes if you stopped blinking?
A thought experiment. This is not medical advice.

The facts:
- People blink about 15 to 20 times a minute (American Academy of Ophthalmology).
- Each blink spreads the tear film, which keeps the front of the eye moist and smooth and washes away dust.
- In a healthy eye, the tear film usually starts to break up after about 10 seconds without blinking (tear break-up time).
- Dry spots cause blurry vision, stinging and redness. If the eye stays exposed for a long time, the cornea can be damaged (exposure keratopathy).
Sources: American Academy of Ophthalmology ("Why do we blink?"); Cleveland Clinic ("Dry Eye"); clinical definitions of tear break-up time.
